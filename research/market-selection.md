# 🌍 African Fintech Market Selection Framework

## Objective

Identify which African markets should be prioritized for fintech expansion using a structured, data-driven framework.

## Evaluation Criteria

Markets are evaluated across six dimensions:

| Factor                   | Weight |
| ------------------------ | -----: |
| Market Opportunity       |    25% |
| Digital Payment Adoption |    20% |
| Customer Need            |    20% |
| Competitive Opportunity  |    15% |
| Regulatory Feasibility   |    10% |
| Partnership Potential    |    10% |

## Scoring

Each factor receives a score from **1 to 5**.

**1 = Very Low**

**2 = Low**

**3 = Moderate**

**4 = High**

**5 = Very High**

The weighted score provides a standardized way to compare markets.

## Example Markets

The framework can be applied to:

* Nigeria
* Ghana
* Kenya
* Uganda
* South Africa
* Egypt
* Tanzania
* Côte d'Ivoire
* Senegal

## Decision Framework

A high score does not automatically mean a market should be entered.

The final decision should also consider:

* Regulatory requirements
* Product readiness
* Available capital
* Local partnerships
* Operational complexity
* Competitive dynamics

## Recommended Decision

Markets should be classified as:

**Priority 1 — Enter**

Strong opportunity with manageable execution requirements.

**Priority 2 — Validate**

Promising market requiring additional research or partnerships.

**Priority 3 — Monitor**

Potential future opportunity but not an immediate priority.

**Do Not Enter**

Insufficient opportunity or excessive execution risk.

## Important

This framework is designed for portfolio demonstration. Actual market-entry decisions require current market research, regulatory assessment and primary customer research.
