# 💡 Strategic Recommendations

## Overview

The market-scoring framework provides a structured approach for evaluating African markets for potential fintech expansion.

The scores in this portfolio are **illustrative** and are intended to demonstrate how a commercial market-prioritization model can work.

## Strategic Approach

A fintech should avoid selecting markets based solely on population size or overall economic activity.

The preferred market should demonstrate a combination of:

* Strong customer need
* Digital-payment adoption
* Commercial opportunity
* Partnership potential
* Competitive differentiation
* Manageable regulatory requirements

## Recommended Expansion Process

### 1. Prioritize

Identify markets with the strongest overall scores.

### 2. Validate

Conduct primary research with customers, merchants and potential partners.

### 3. Pilot

Launch a focused product offering in one market and one customer segment.

### 4. Measure

Track customer acquisition, activation, retention, transaction volume and operational performance.

### 5. Optimize

Use customer feedback and performance data to improve the product and go-to-market strategy.

### 6. Scale

Expand investment only after the pilot demonstrates sustainable product-market fit.

## Commercial Considerations

Market attractiveness alone does not guarantee successful expansion.

A market may have strong demand but still present challenges related to:

* Regulation
* Infrastructure
* Competition
* Foreign exchange
* Customer acquisition cost
* Payment reliability
* Local partnerships

Therefore, market scoring should be combined with qualitative research and commercial due diligence.

## Key Takeaway

The objective of the framework is not to identify a single "best" African market.

Instead, it creates a repeatable decision-making process that helps a fintech answer:

> **Where should we expand, why should we expand there, and what evidence would justify scaling?**
